About Me

I am a hardworking, dedicated, and resilient individual with a National Diploma in Computer Engineering and currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science. While I naturally have an introverted personality, I am a focused listener, analytical thinker, and someone who enjoys solving technical problems.

For over ten years, I have been working toward becoming a software engineer. My journey has not been straightforward, as I often moved between different technologies and areas of interest before developing the discipline and clarity needed to focus on software engineering. These experiences have taught me perseverance, adaptability, and the importance of continuous learning.

Alongside my learning journey, I have gained practical work experience in IT support and network infrastructure while balancing professional responsibilities with self-study. I am passionate about backend development, artificial intelligence, and building software solutions that solve real-world problems. I am now committed to becoming a highly skilled software engineer and look forward to contributing my technical knowledge, strong work ethic, and determination to an organization where I can continue to grow and make a meaningful impact.
